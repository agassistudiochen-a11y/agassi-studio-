Visual Identity for Events

Three events, three worlds. Each identity begins as a film, then extends into a key visual and the formats a night actually needs — proof that the system holds from a teaser reel to a lineup story.

ROLE
Concept · Art Direction · Motion
DISCIPLINES
Event Identity · Teaser Film · Key Art
SCOPE
Three events
The Approach

An event lives or dies on its first three seconds. I start each identity as a film — set the mood in motion — then pull the key visual and the platform pieces out of that same world so everything feels like one night.
