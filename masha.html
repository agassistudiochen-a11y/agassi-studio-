02 — Case Study

MASHA

A self-initiated fictional brand built around the idea of time worn as adornment. What began as a design-school project grew into a complete identity, campaign system and two short films.

Role
Concept · Brand Direction · Art Direction
Disciplines
Identity · Campaign · Visual System · Film
Year
2025

The Idea

I started with a question: if a brand made objects out of time itself, who would wear them? The answer became a cast of clock-faced figures and a world where time behaves like status, ornament and obsession. The identity, imagery and films all grew from that premise.
